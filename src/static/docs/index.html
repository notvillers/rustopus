#1 TRANSLATE

HUNGARIAN IN, ENGLISH OUT

FETCHES OCTOPUS 8 ERP SOAP PAYLOADS AND RETURNS THEM AS ENGLISH-TAGGED XML. PASS language=hu TO KEEP THE ORIGINAL HUNGARIAN.

#2 CONVERT

XML, CSV OR XLSX

EVERY SUPPORTED ENDPOINT CAN ALSO ANSWER IN SEMICOLON-DELIMITED CSV OR IN XLSX — JUST ADD data_type={desired_type}.

#3 AGGREGATE

CALL ONCE, GET EVERYTHING

/get-bulkCOMPOSES PRODUCTS, PRICES, STOCKS, IMAGES AND BARCODES INTO A SINGLE RESPONSE WITH PER-CALL FALLBACK ERRORS.

#4 MCP

ASK YOUR AI BETA

/mcp LETS AN AI ASSISTANT SEARCH PRODUCTS, PRICES AND STOCK, READ INVOICES, EXPORT TO XLSX OR CSV AND — WHEN ENABLED — PLACE ORDERS. SIGN IN VIA OAUTH WITH YOUR OCTOPUS AUTHCODE.
